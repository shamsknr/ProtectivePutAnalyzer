Strategy Logic
01

Buy Delivery

Take a long delivery position expecting the stock to rise over 1–2 months.

02

Buy Put Option

Pay the premium to lock a price floor. If the stock falls, your put gains value and offsets losses.

03

Capped Downside

Max loss is fixed regardless of how far the stock falls. Brokerage is factored into every P&L shown.
